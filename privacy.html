Just-in-time consentIf Fin needs information you have not approved, it asks first: “Allow once”, “Always allow” or “Not now”. Nothing is sent silently.

When you use Fin, AI categorisation or a monthly summary, only the figures needed for that request are sent to an AI service (OpenRouter). Track My Fin does not store them on a server.
