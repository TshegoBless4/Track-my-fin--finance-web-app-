How does this work?

Each category has a monthly limit that you set (it repeats every month until you change it). Spent is the money that went out in that category during the month shown above — it comes from the transactions you add or import on Overview. Used so far adds up all the spending, Still available is your total limits minus that, and Safe to spend divides what is left by the days remaining this month. Seeing R0? Check the month (use the arrows), and that your imported expenses are negative amounts.
